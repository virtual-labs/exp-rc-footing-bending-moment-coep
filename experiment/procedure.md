## PROCEDURE
 
-	Enter the working Load (P) in kN
-	Enter working moment (M) in kNm
-	Select grade of concrete (f<sub>ck</sub>) in N/mm<sup>2</sup> and grade of steel (fy) in N/mm<sup>2</sup>
-	Enter the column dimensions l and b in mm
-	Select the type of strata and then select the SBC of soil in KN/m<sup>2</sup>
-	Enter the effective cover (d') in mm
-	Once all the basic design parameters are entered, click on Next level.
-	Enter self weight of footing (%) in the range of 10 to 15.
-	Calculate the area of footing (A<sub>f</sub>) in m<sup>2</sup>. After three incorrect attempts the correct formula and answer will be displayed.
-	Then enter the length of footing in m within the range of √A<sub>f</sub> to √1.5A<sub>f</sub>
-	Proceed to the further step by clicking on ‘Next Level’
-	Calculate the net upward pressure in both longer and shorter directions. After three incorrect attempts the correct formula and answer will be displayed.
-	In the next step, calculate Mux and Muy in Nmm and Astx and Asty in mm<sup>2</sup>. After three incorrect attempts the correct formula and answer will be displayed.
-	The minimum area of steel required will be calculated and the maximum value will be considered for further calculations.
-	Choose bar diameter for reinforcement in both directions. Lastly, spacing will be calculated.
